## What to drop into `assets/` (any missing file becomes a labelled placeholder)
- `clips/`: gta.mp4, skate.mp4, halo.mp4, darksouls.mp4, league.mp4, zombies.mp4, overgrowth.mp4 (your Cmd+Shift+5 capture)
- `tweets/`: main.png, reply1.png, reply2.png, reply3.png (screenshot the tweet and its best replies)
- `sfx/`: record_scratch, vine_boom, whoosh, ding, bruh, airhorn, dun_dun, sad_trombone, alarm, round_change, pop, villager, riser, click (.wav or .mp3)
- `music/`: funny.mp3 (upbeat, funky), zombies.mp3 (dark, alarmed)

**Where to get sounds legally:** Pixabay Sound Effects and Music, the YouTube Audio Library, Freesound (CC0 filter), or Epidemic Sound if you have it. Most famous meme sounds (vine boom, bruh, airhorn) have free soundalikes on Pixabay. For other people's game clips, use short excerpts and credit the creator on screen; the script adds a credit tag to every clip.
